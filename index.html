"Market Value" as defined by the Appraisal Institute of Canada is the most probable price, as of a specified date, in cash, or in terms equivalent to cash, or in other precisely revealed terms, for which the specified property rights should sell after reasonable exposure in a competitive market under all conditions requisite to a fair sale, with the buyer and seller each acting prudently, knowledgeably, and for self-interest, and assuming that neither is under undue duress.

In submitting this report, I certify that:

  1. I have no interest, present or contemplated in the above property
