My two mantras

Create once,
publish everywhere.

The Gary Vee approach. One piece of content, every platform.

ii.

Less time on what I hate.
More time for what I love.

AI takes the tasks I put off. I keep the people.

The core idea: do your admin by voice while you drive. Talk it out, and the drafts are waiting when you park.

My two tools

Claude

  • Heavy lifting on coding
  • More connectors
  • Familiar chat, like ChatGPT
  • Claude in Chrome navigates sites for you

Grok

  • Most functional day to day
  • Set up like a team of employees
  • Own browser: log in with your credentials, no API keys
  • Talk to it by voice
Grok example: I trained it to put documents into my transaction system (Sisu), so I don't have to.
