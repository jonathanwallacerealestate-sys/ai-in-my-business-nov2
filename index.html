Grok

  • Most functional day to day

What I dropped
  • Building too big, too fast
  • Everything connected to everything
  • Piles of scheduled automations that broke and burned money
  • Managing a thousand data points instead of producing
Lesson: stay organized as an agent. Maximize output on the tasks you dislike.
