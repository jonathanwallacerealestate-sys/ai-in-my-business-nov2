Best practices · keep your AI on track

Correct once, save it forever

  1. It does not remember on its own. Give it a file.
  2. A correction you only say once is gone. Save it.
  3. Before you close a chat, ask it to write up any new step-by-step and any decision you agreed.Save that to your rules file, so the next chat starts smarter.
End of each chat
Before we finish, list any new step-by-step processes and decisions from this chat in short bullet points so I can save them to my rules file.

While you are learning

What not to waste time with

  • Don't subscribe to every tool. Pick one and learn it.
  • Don't chase every new app.
  • Don't spend hours perfecting a prompt. Talk to it, then correct it once and save the correction.
  • Don't build automations or custom bots on day one.
  • Don't let it send anything before you have read it.
  • Don't trust it for MLS facts, prices, or legal wording without checking.
  • Don't paste private client information in before your rules are in place.

Q&A

Which tool, and do I pay?

The one you will open every day. Free is enough to start. About $50 to $100 a month covers the tools when you need them.

Mistakes, and sending

Check prices, dates, and legal wording. You read it. You send it. Anything client-facing gets your eyes first.

Client information

Keep it out. Use Sample. No ID, banking, or mortgage numbers. Connecting tools is Step 2.
