01 · How to start

How to start: one assistant, every day

One task you hate

Pick one low-value task you put off. Use the chat for that one thing.

Write your rules down

Name, brokerage, area, and how you write. Paste that note at the start of the chat.

Drafts only

It drafts. You review. You send. Correct it once, and keep that line.

This week: a free account and the phone app. Grok is the one I open most. ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, or Copilot will do. Use your one task on 3 or more days.

02 · Where to focus · calls and meetings

Record the call. Let AI do the notes.

  1. A Plaud recorder clips to the back of the phone and records the call or the meeting.
  2. The Plaud app turns the recording into a transcript and a summary.
  3. AI reads that summary and drafts a CRM note, the tasks and dates, and a follow-up email or text.It recaps what the client said, and what happens next.
  4. You read it, fix anything, and you send it.
Let people know you are recording. You stay present, and the next call picks up where this one stopped.

Best practices · keep your AI on track

It learns only what you save

  1. It does not remember on its own. Give it a place to keep things.
  2. A correction you only say once is gone. Save it.
  3. One written rules file.House rules, or the AI's memory, custom instructions, or project instructions.
  4. Keep it short and specific. Date each rule. The newest rule wins.
Correct once, save it forever.

House rules · example

  • Canadian spelling. Short paragraphs. 10 Nov 2026.
  • Warm, plain words. My sign-off, every time. 10 Nov 2026.
  • Never send without my approval. Copy me on the draft. 10 Nov 2026.
  • If a fact is missing, say so. Do not guess. 10 Nov 2026.
Best practices · sources, then drift

Point it at the right source

1. Your own workTemplates and posts you have already approved.
2. BrokerageYour forms and your brokerage rules.
3. Official sourcesRECO, and your board.
4. The open webLast. Tell it to say when something is missing.
  1. Start each task by pointing at the rules file and the template.
  2. Reuse a saved kit. Show an example of a good result.
  3. When it drifts, correct it and update the rules file.
  4. One source of truth. No second copy that disagrees.
  5. Review a sample before anything goes out.
  6. When a chat gets long and muddled, start a fresh one. Ask it to read your rules back.

04 · What not to waste time with

What not to waste time with

  • Don't subscribe to every tool. Pick one and learn it.
  • Don't chase every new app or announcement.
  • Don't spend hours perfecting prompts. Talk to it like an assistant, then correct it.
  • Don't build complex automations or custom bots on day one.
  • Don't let it send anything to a client before you have read it.
  • Don't trust it for MLS facts, prices, or legal and contract wording without checking.
  • Don't paste private client information into a tool you have not set up with your rules.
