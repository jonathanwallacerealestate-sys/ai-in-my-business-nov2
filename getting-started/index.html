Notes, emails, your day

  • Messy list first
  • The follow-up
  • Check the original

Your lead (CRM record)

Call or text, email on file

Stage: Hot. A likely seller who came in through the team.

Last contact: Nobody has called yet. Only a template email and your newsletter.

Why now: Seller tags on the file, never reached, and no listing or sale on the property since last year.

Angle: Check in on their plans for the house, and offer an updated market value.

Copy and paste

Text

  1. Hi [Name], [Your name] here. Prices on your street have shifted this year. Want me to send you an updated value for your home? No pressure either way.
  2. Hi [Name], quick one from [Your name]. Are you still planning anything with the house in 2026 or 2027? Happy to help whenever the timing is right.
  3. Hi [Name], [Your name] here. Before the holidays get busy, are you thinking about a spring move? Winter is a great time to get ready, and I'm happy to send what's selling near you.

Email

  1. Subject: An updated value for your home Hi [Name], a lot has changed in the market since you first reached out. I can put together an updated value for your home at no cost. Just reply and I'll get started. [Your name]
  2. Subject: Still thinking about 2026? Hi [Name], are you still planning anything with the house in the next year or two? Even if it's early, I can share what's selling nearby so you can plan. [Your name]
  3. Subject: Getting ready for a spring move Hi [Name], spring is the busiest time to sell, and the sellers who do best start planning over the winter. If the house is on your mind for 2026, I can send an updated value and a simple prep list. Enjoy the season, [Your name]
