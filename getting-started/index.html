01 · How to start

How to start: one assistant, every day

One task you hate

Pick one low-value task you put off. Use the chat for that one thing.

Write your rules down

Name, brokerage, area, and how you write. Paste that note at the start of the chat.

Drafts only

It drafts. You review. You send. Correct it once, and keep that line.

This week: a free account and the phone app. Grok is the one I open most. ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, or Copilot will do. Use your one task on 3 or more days.
01 · How to start · sample data only

Live demo: from messy notes to a ready-to-send draft

Sample prompt 
I'm a REALTOR® in Midland, Ontario. Here are my messy notes from a showing today. SAMPLE DATA ONLY.

Buyers: Alex and Jordan Sample. Saw 123 Maple Sample St. Loved the kitchen and the walk to the waterfront trail. Worried the yard is small for their dog. Asked about school bus routes and what similar homes sold for. They're pre-approved, want to decide in 2 weeks.

Please give me:
1. A short, friendly follow-up email I can send tonight (under 120 words, no em dashes, no exclamation marks).
2. Three questions to ask them on our next call.
3. A to-do list for me before that call, marking anything I need to verify myself.
Do not invent sale prices or school facts. Say "verify" where I need to check.
Backup example · if Wi-Fi fails 
BACKUP EXAMPLE. Not a live answer.
Fictional clients: Alex and Jordan Sample.
123 Maple Sample St is sample data, not a real home.

1. Email
Hi Alex and Jordan,

Thank you for walking through 123 Maple Sample St with me today. You loved the kitchen and the walk to the waterfront trail. You are also worried the yard is small for your dog.

You are pre-approved and want to decide in two weeks. I will verify school bus routes and what similar homes sold for before our next call. I will not guess at either.

Jonathan Wallace, REALTOR®

2. Questions
- What would the yard need for your dog to be comfortable?
- Which school bus details should I verify before we talk?
- Once similar sales are verified, what else do you need in order to decide?

3. To-do before the call
- Verify school bus routes for this address. Do not guess.
- Verify what similar homes sold for. Do not invent prices.
- Confirm the yard size from the listing or a second look.
- Read the draft, fix one line if needed, and send it yourself.

02 · Where to focus your efforts

Emails, texts and follow-up

  • Post-showing thank-you. A check-in with a past client. A "still looking?" text.
  • The first draft takes seconds. You edit it, and you send it.
  • For most agents, this serves the people you already have.
This week: copy the draft into the tool you already use. You hit send.
02 · Where to focus your efforts

Summaries, prep, and buyer research

Summaries

A long email, condo status certificate notes, or call notes. No private client identifiers. Key points, risks, and questions. Then check the original.

Appointment prep

A one-page sheet for a first buyer consultation. Sample: a couple relocating to Midland.

Buyers

Neighbourhood questions, a showing-day checklist, the follow-up, and a plain-language explainer. Example: what a home inspection condition means. Verify every fact.

Search stays in MLS

The chat prepares questions and summaries. It does not replace your search.

04 · What not to waste time with

What not to waste time with

  • Don't subscribe to every tool. Pick one and learn it.
  • Don't chase every new app or announcement.
  • Don't spend hours perfecting prompts. Talk to it like an assistant, then correct it.
  • Don't build complex automations or custom bots on day one.
  • Don't let it send anything to a client before you have read it.
  • Don't trust it for MLS facts, prices, or legal and contract wording without checking.
  • Don't paste private client information into a tool you have not set up with your rules.
